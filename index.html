How it works

  1. Open an EPUB. The app finds the chapters and flags contents, copyright and "Also by" pages so you can untick them.
  2. Pick a voice and language. Microsoft's free edge-tts neural voices, no API key. Chinese books get a Mandarin voice automatically; switch to a Hong Kong voice for Cantonese.
  3. Convert. Each chapter renders to its own MP3, written safely so an interrupted run resumes where it stopped.
  4. Listen and read along. The built-in player highlights each sentence using the timing edge-tts sends with the audio. Or export a single .m4b with chapter markers.

Six versions

V1
Extraction, chapter detection, synthesis, atomic writes, resume
V2
Shared engine, timing sidecars for read-along, ID3 tags and .m4b
V3–4
Read-along timed directly from edge-tts boundary events, speed control, the modern interface
V5
Mandarin and Cantonese voices, Chinese sentence splitting and fonts
V6
Finds ffmpeg off PATH, so speed control and .m4b work on a fresh machine
About the books. The app ships no books or audio. Convert e-books you own or public-domain titles, for personal listening. The text is sent to Microsoft's edge-tts service to be voiced; everything else runs on your computer.
